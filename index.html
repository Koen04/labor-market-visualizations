U.S. Workforce Aging and Labor Market Projections

An exploratory analysis combining 2024 American Community Survey (ACS) microdata with Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) occupational projections for 2024–2034.

Examines the relationship between the share of workers aged 55+ and projected occupational employment growth.

Methodology

ACS microdata were aggregated using person-level survey weights. BLS projections were mapped to comparable ACS occupational categories using the NEM–ACS crosswalk. The matched sample represents approximately 98.09% of weighted ACS employment.

Occupational openings are projections of hiring opportunities, not direct measures of unfilled vacancies or labor shortages.

Tools: Python, Pandas, Plotly, ACS PUMS, BLS Employment Projections.
